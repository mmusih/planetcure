Kweneng International

Parent app

A closer connection to your child's school day.

Kweneng International helps parents and guardians of Kweneng International Secondary School students keep up with school life, access their child's records and communicate with the school.

Learning & attendance

View timetables, homework, attendance, academic results and report cards.

School updates

Read announcements, follow school events, access documents and exchange messages with the school.

Everyday school matters

Check fee balances and receipts, submit absence notices and update student and emergency contact information.

Getting access

This app is for parents and guardians of enrolled students. Register using the parent code supplied by the school, or sign in with your existing parent account. Contact the school if you need help with your code or account.

Parent support & privacy enquiries

For help with the app, your account, personal information or an account-deletion enquiry, contact the school at:

admin@kwenenginternational.com

Include the app name and a brief description of your request. Do not email your password, identity documents or medical records.
